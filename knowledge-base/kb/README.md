# kb — the curated knowledge

Pages written by the agent from the cards and the sources, under the rules of `AGENTS.md`: every factual statement
footnoted to a passage (`M03¶12`) or under a heading `### Inferred`; one home per fact; no word limit.

The page types are not fixed in advance. A type gets its own folder when its third page would be written. Types that
have served research projects: a **primer** (how one part of the subject works, for a newcomer), a **concept** (one
term or component), a **decision** (a choice, its stated and its inferred reasons, the alternatives, what would make it
reconsidered), an **evidence** record (one measured or simulated result and what it rests on), and registers of
**assumptions** (every number with its basis), **open questions**, and **people by role**. `index.md` lists every page.
